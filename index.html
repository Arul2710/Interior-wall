Our Creative Process

A clear, collaborative journey from the first conversation to the final brushstroke.

  1. 01

    Discovery Call

    We listen to your ideas, understand the room's purpose, and define the mood you want to create.

  2. 02

    Site Visit & Measurements

    We assess the wall surface, lighting, and proportions so the artwork fits your space perfectly.

  3. 03

    Concept & Color Study

    Sketches and palette studies are shared with you and refined until every detail feels right.

  4. 04

    Hand-Painted Execution

    Our artists bring the design to life with premium, non-toxic paints applied in careful layers.

  5. 05

    Final Walkthrough & Care

    We review the finished wall together and share simple care tips to keep it looking fresh for years.
