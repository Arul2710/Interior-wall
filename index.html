Customization Options

Made Entirely Yours

Every mural is configured around six decisions we make together, so the finished artwork fits your space perfectly.

Style & Theme

Abstract, botanical, geometric, landscape, portraiture or brand-led illustration.

Colour Palette

We colour-match to your furniture, brand guide or a mood you bring to us.

Scale & Composition

Designed to your exact wall dimensions, sightlines and furniture layout.

Finish & Texture

Matte, satin, metallic, textured plaster or gold-leaf accents on request.

Content & Typography

Quotes, logos, wayfinding and signage integrated into the artwork.
